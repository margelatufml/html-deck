The Honest Gap

Eurostat’s own page does not break out Romania’s under-40 farm-manager share — only the EU-27 figure above. Press estimates for Romania exist; none is primary-sourced, so none is printed here.

Two in five Romanian farms are run by someone past retirement age. That is the succession question, and nobody has answered it.
