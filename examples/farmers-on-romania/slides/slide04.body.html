The Trap

The Average Farm Got Bigger. Nothing Consolidated.

Romania’s average holding rose from 3.45 ha to 4.42 ha between 2010 and 2020 — up 28%. It is a real number, and it means the opposite of what it looks like.

+28%

Read as a Press Release

3.45 ha → 4.42 ha
INS, 2010–2020

Average holding size up 28% in a decade. Read the way a ministry release would: farms consolidating, Romania converging toward the EU norm.

−33%

What Actually Happened

1.4 million farms gone
Eurostat, 2005–2023

A 33% decline — the largest of any EU country. The average rose because the bottom of the distribution was deleted, not because anyone merged fields.

A mean is the wrong statistic for a distribution this skewed — a few estates and millions of micro-plots do not average to a “typical” farm.

The average went up because the smallest farms stopped existing. That is not consolidation. It is subtraction.
