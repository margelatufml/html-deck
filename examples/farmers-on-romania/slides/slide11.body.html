The Counter-Argument

Four Things This Deck Is Probably Getting Wrong

Every slide so far has an argument built into it. Here is the case against each one, which an informed room would make anyway.

1

“Small equals backward.”

90.2% of Romanian holdings work under 5 ha (Eurostat, 2023) — many are semi-subsistence plots feeding rural households directly, not failed businesses. Consolidation carries a real social cost, rural depopulation and lost livelihoods, that an efficiency framing simply elides.

2

“Romania is short-changed on CAP.”

The low per-hectare rate comes out of convergence formulas fixed at 2007 accession, not a present-day decision. Romania is also shifting rural-development funds into direct payments and adding €978.7 million of redistributive support for farms under 50 ha (EC, 2023–2027).

3

“The trade deficit proves farming is failing.”

The agri-food deficit widened 50% in a year, €3.2bn to €4.8bn (INS, via Agroberichten Buitenland, 2024) — equally consistent with rising household incomes buying more processed food. It is evidence about value-added capacity, not a verdict on the farm sector.

4

“2024 shows the real picture.”

The 63% maize-yield collapse (Eurostat, apro_cpsh1, 2018 & 2024) compares a decade best to an explicit drought outlier. A multi-year average is less alarming, and more honest, than picking the worst year and calling it structural.

If a deck only contains evidence that supports it, it is an argument wearing statistics. These four are the ones that would be thrown back at me, so they are on the slide.
