How Many Romanians Work in Agriculture?

One question, one country, one year. Three official answers that differ by a factor of two.

Eurostat · National Accounts

20.7%
Eurostat, national accounts · 2023

The highest share of employment in agriculture of any EU member state, against an EU average of 3.9% (Eurostat, 2023).

MEASURES SOMETHING ELSE

INS · Labour Force Survey

11.9%
INS Labour Force Survey · 2023

Romania’s own statistics institute, the same country, the same year — counted a different way (INS Labour Force Survey, 2023).

MEASURES SOMETHING ELSE

European Commission · DG AGRI

23%
EC DG AGRI, CAP country page · undated

The Commission’s own CAP country page carries no year at all, so this number cannot be pinned to 2023 or any other (European Commission DG AGRI, undated).

MEASURES SOMETHING ELSE

They are not contradicting each other — they count different things: national-accounts employment, a household labour-force survey, and (most likely) annual work units. But you cannot average them, and you cannot quietly pick the one that suits the argument.

Before anyone argues about agricultural policy, they should have to say which of these three numbers they mean. Almost nobody does.
