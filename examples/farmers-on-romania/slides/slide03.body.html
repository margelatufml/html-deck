The Micro-Holding

2.6 million holdings under 5 hectares (Eurostat, 2023) — mostly household plots, not commercial farms. Nine in ten Romanian farms belong here, and together they work roughly a quarter of the country’s farmland.

2.6M holdings · Eurostat, 2023

The Estate

One in a hundred Romanian farms is 50 hectares or larger (Eurostat, 2023) — and that one holding in a hundred works 53.1% of all the agricultural land in the country (Eurostat, 2023).

1.0% of farms · 53.1% of land · Eurostat, 2023

Nine in ten farms work a quarter of the land. One in a hundred works half of it. Any sentence beginning ‘the Romanian farmer’ is about one of these two and not the other.
