The counter-evidence, said before anyone raises it: 2024 was an explicit drought outlier. Using it as the representative year overstates the structural weakness. The point is the range, not the low.

The same fields produced 7.64 tonnes a hectare and 2.83 tonnes a hectare six years apart. That spread, not the average, is what a farmer actually plans against.
