A Third of Europe’s Farms

Romania has 2.9 million agricultural holdings — 32.6% of the EU’s 8.8 million (Eurostat, 2023). It is the most-quoted fact about Romanian agriculture and it explains almost nothing.

2.9 million of the EU’s 8.8 million agricultural holdings (Eurostat, 2023)
Same country, measured by land instead of farm count: 12.6 million ha utilised agricultural area — 8.0% of the EU total (Eurostat, 2023).

The puncture: a third of the farms, working an eighth of the land. The number tells you nothing about who farms, how much they work, or whether they sell anything at all.

One in three European farms is Romanian. That is a fact about counting, not about farming.
