The counter-evidence, said before anyone raises it: a widening food deficit is also what a catching-up economy looks like when household incomes rise and people buy more processed and premium food. It is evidence about value-added capacity — it is not on its own a verdict on the farm sector.

Weakest source in this deck: INS data reported via Agroberichten Buitenland — a secondary site, not a primary INS or Eurostat page.
