Ask Which Number They Mean

No recap. Three claims worth carrying out, four things still unanswered, and one question to ask the next time a number goes by.

Three Things Worth Carrying Out
1

One in three EU farms is Romanian (Eurostat, 2023); nine in ten of them work roughly a quarter of the land (INS, 2020).

2

The average farm grew because the smallest farms disappeared (Eurostat, 2005–2023), not because anything consolidated.

3

Two in five farm managers are over 65 (Eurostat, 2023), and nobody has a sourced succession number.

What I Still Do Not Know

Romania’s own under-40 farm-manager share — Eurostat does not publish it by country on the page used here.
Whether the €147/ha figure survives contact with a primary EC source — the only source found is the Thünen Institute, a secondary one.
What share of the 2.6 million micro-holdings (Eurostat, 2023) sell anything at all — the only figures found were from 2013, on a secondary site.
Whether any of this changes once 2025 and 2026 are finalised.

The Ask

“Next time someone quotes a number about Romanian farming, ask them which of the three it is and what year it refers to. Most of the time there is no answer.”
